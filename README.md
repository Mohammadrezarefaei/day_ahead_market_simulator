## 📖 Overview

The **Day-Ahead Market Clearing & Alpine Hydro Simulator** is an interactive engineering and economic application designed to model day-ahead electricity markets. Built with Streamlit and powered by SciPy's advanced `linprog` (HiGHS) solver, this application optimizes social welfare by efficiently matching supply generators with demand blocks, simulating real-world market clearing dynamics and cross-border energy flows.

**🔗 [Live Application / Interactive Dashboard](https://dayaheadmarketsimulator-lstichryakpvpcn7qobps5.streamlit.app/)**

## ✨ Key Features

*   **Robust Market Optimization:** Utilizes SciPy's HiGHS linear programming solver for highly stable, crash-free social welfare maximization.
*   **Interactive Parameters:** Dynamically adjust the number of supply generators and demand blocks via a clean sidebar UI.
*   **Visual Market Dynamics:** Automatically generates and plots standard Supply & Demand step-curves, visually identifying the Market Clearing Price (MCP) and Cleared Volume.
*   **Real-time Metrics:** Instantly calculates and displays Total Social Welfare, MCP, and Total Cleared Volume.
*   **Data Export:** Bundles all cleared supply and demand bids, alongside high-resolution plots, into a downloadable `.zip` archive.
